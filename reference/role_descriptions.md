# Role descriptions

One summary per target role. Used for résumé variants and the fit-check brief.

## AI Engineer

AI Engineer with 3.5+ years production experience building multi-agent LLM systems and agentic infrastructure. Specialized in LangGraph orchestration, heterogeneous LLM routing, RAG pipelines, and LLM fine-tuning, deployed at production scale.

## ML Engineer

ML Engineer with 3.5+ years production experience deploying agentic systems, fine-tuned LLMs, and scalable ML pipelines. Specialized in multi-agent LangGraph architectures, model compression, and time-series classification, shipped end-to-end from data pipeline to production deployment.

## Data Scientist

Data Scientist with 3.5+ years production experience building statistical models and ML pipelines at scale. Specialized in classification modeling, time-series forecasting, and KPI analytics frameworks, translating data into measurable business decisions.
